Studio Uploader

A personal desktop tool

Studio Uploader is a command-line tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It lets the owner sign in to their own TikTok accounts with TikTok Login Kit and send short videos they made themselves to their TikTok inbox as drafts, through the Content Posting API. The owner then finishes and posts each draft in the TikTok app. It also reads the public statistics of those accounts' own videos.

It is not a public service: nobody else can sign up for it or use it, and it never acts on anyone else's account.
