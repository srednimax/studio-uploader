Privacy Policy

Studio Uploader · Last updated 12 October 2026

Who is responsible

Maksymilian Średniawa operates Studio Uploader and is responsible for this data. Contact: sredniamx@gmail.com.

Whose data

Studio Uploader connects only to TikTok accounts owned by the operator. It does not serve other users, and it does not collect data about the people who watch, like or comment on the videos.

What it accesses from TikTok

  • Basic profile information of the operator's connected account (open ID, username, display name). This is used to confirm that the right account is connected.
  • The operator's own videos, which it uploads to the account's inbox as drafts, and their upload status.
  • Public information and statistics about the account's own videos (for example views, likes, comments, shares). These are used to plan future content.
  • The access and refresh tokens TikTok issues when the operator signs in.

Where it is kept

All of this is stored only on the operator's own computer. Tokens are kept in a file only the operator can read. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.

Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until access is revoked or they expire. Video statistics are kept for as long as they are useful for planning. Revoking the app's access in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) stops all further access. To have stored data deleted, email the address above.

Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
